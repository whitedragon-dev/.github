# Rebrand Notice: WhiteDragon is now WhiteDragon-software

**Effective date:** 1 October 2026

We are pleased to announce that **WhiteDragon** has officially rebranded to **WhiteDragon-software**.

This change reflects the evolution of our organization from a collection of individual projects into a unified software collective. While our name and identity have been updated, our mission remains unchanged: building minimal, readable, open software for the open web — few dependencies, no build step, and small enough to understand in full.

## What's changing

* **Organization name:** WhiteDragon → WhiteDragon-software
* **GitHub organization:** All repositories, issues, and discussions have moved to [github.com/whitedragon-software](https://github.com/whitedragon-software)

* **Branding:** Logos, documentation, and package metadata will be updated progressively

## What's not changing

- **Our projects:** `relay`, `daybreak`, `webcore`, `whitedragon-authenticator`, and `whitedragon-forum` continue as before, with active development
- **Our licenses:** WD-NCL v1.0 and WD-SAL v1.0 remain in effect for existing releases
- **Our commitment:** Minimal software, transparent code, and no unnecessary dependencies

## For contributors and users

* **Update your bookmarks and remotes:** If you have the old organization URL saved or cloned, please update to `github.com/whitedragon-software`
* **Existing links:** Old links will redirect where possible, but we recommend updating references at your earliest convenience
* **Questions?** Reach out via our [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/whitedragon-software/whitedragon-forum) or open an issue in the relevant repository

We appreciate your continued support and look forward to building the next chapter as **WhiteDragon-software**.

— The WhiteDragon-software team
